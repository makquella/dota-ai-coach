# Code of conduct

Wardly follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work, and do not harass anyone, in issues, pull requests, the Telegram channel or anywhere the project is represented.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [makquella](https://github.com/makquella), through [GitHub](https://github.com/makquella). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
