# Items

## Farming Items

### Battle Fury

Battle Fury is the primary farming item for melee carries such as Anti-Mage and Juggernaut. It provides a cleave AoE that hits all nearby units, allowing the carry to clear jungle camps and lane waves in one swing. Prioritise this item if your hero relies on split-pushing or farming multiple camps simultaneously.

### Maelstrom / Mjollnir

Maelstrom grants a chain lightning proc on attacks, enabling efficient farm across multiple targets. It is particularly strong on heroes with high attack speed (Luna, Juggernaut) or those who need to clear wave-jungle combos quickly. Mjollnir upgrades Maelstrom and adds Static Charge for team fights.

### Radiance

Radiance provides an AoE burn that deals continuous damage to all nearby enemies. It is a strong farming item for heroes who can stack ancients or fight near grouped enemies (Naga Siren, Spectre). Avoid Radiance on heroes who prefer direct confrontation.

## Survivability Items

### Black King Bar (BKB)

BKB grants spell immunity for 6–10 seconds depending on usage count. It is the most important fighting item for carries who are vulnerable to disables. Buy BKB before Aghanim if the enemy has multiple stuns, silences, or hexes.

### Linken's Sphere

Linken's Sphere blocks one targeted spell every 14 seconds. It is effective against single-point-of-failure ultimates (Doom, Fiend's Grip, Laguna Blade). It provides good regeneration stats for the laning phase.

### Eye of Skadi

Eye of Skadi reduces enemy movement speed and attack speed on hit. It provides large HP, mana, and all-stat bonuses, making it one of the best survivability-and-damage hybrids for late-game carries.

### Heart of Tarrasque

Heart of Tarrasque provides the highest raw HP in the game along with regeneration. It transforms a squishy carry into a durable frontliner capable of absorbing multiple ultimates. Buy Heart when the enemy has high burst damage but limited sustained DPS.

## Damage Items

### Daedalus

Daedalus provides a 30% critical strike chance for 225% damage. It is the highest raw DPS item in the game and is core on most physical damage carries in the late game (Anti-Mage, Luna, Juggernaut).

### Butterfly

Butterfly provides the highest agility bonus in the game along with 35% evasion and attack speed. It is a strong damage and survivability hybrid on agility carries. Evasion is particularly effective against right-click heavy lineups.

### Satanic

Satanic provides lifesteal and on-activation grants 175% lifesteal for 3.5 seconds. It allows carries to sustain through prolonged fights and recover from burst damage. Purchase Satanic when you need both damage and fight sustainability.

### Diffusal Blade

Diffusal Blade burns mana on each attack and slows enemy movement speed. It is core on heroes that benefit from mana burn (Juggernaut vs Medusa) or need a slow to stick to targets (Phantom Lancer, Phantom Assassin).
