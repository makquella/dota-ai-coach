# Carry Principles

## Core Decision-Making

A carry player's primary job in the early and mid game is to accumulate gold and experience efficiently. Every death, wasted camp, or unnecessary fight delays your core item timing and directly reduces your team's win probability.

## Farming Priority

Always prioritise farming over fighting unless the fight offers a clear objective reward (tower, Roshan, Barracks). An efficient carry should clear jungle camps between waves and maintain a high last-hit count. Identify the fastest farming route for your hero and stick to it.

## Threat Assessment

Before joining or initiating a fight, ask: what do we win if we win this fight? If the answer is nothing structural — no tower, no Roshan, no buyback advantage — consider skipping the fight and continuing to farm.

## HP Threshold

Never fight below 35% HP unless you are defending your base. At low HP you deal reduced threat and are a free kill for any enemy initiator. Recall to base or retreat to a safe jungle quadrant.

## Enemy Pressure Response

When the enemy applies pressure (aggressive posturing, smoke ganks, or a 5-man push) before minute 18, the correct response is almost always to give up the space and farm safely elsewhere. Your life and items are worth more than a tower at this stage.

## Power Spike Awareness

Know when your hero's power spike occurs. Anti-Mage spikes at Battle Fury completion (~22–25 min). Juggernaut spikes at Blade Fury + Omnislash levels. Luna spikes at Manta Style. Until your spike, avoid extended fights unless forced.

## Objective Timing

Track Roshan timers (spawn at 0:00, respawn 8–11 minutes after death). A Roshan kill combined with a Barracks push can end games. Plan your farm route so you are at full HP and near Roshan when the window opens.

## Positioning in Fights

Stay at maximum attack range. Let your frontline initiate. Step in after the enemy has committed their burst spells. Mispositioned carries are the number-one cause of late-game losses.

## Buyback Awareness

After minute 30, always keep buyback gold available if your team is ahead. Losing a key fight without buyback can result in an immediate throne loss.
