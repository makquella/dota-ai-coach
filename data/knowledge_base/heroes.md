# Heroes

## Anti-Mage

Anti-Mage is a hard carry who scales with farm. His Mana Break passive makes him strong against intelligence heroes, and Blink provides excellent mobility for farming and escaping ganks.

### Anti-Mage Strengths

Anti-Mage can split-push effectively once he has Battle Fury. He farms jungle and lane simultaneously, making him the fastest net-worth carry when left alone.

### Anti-Mage Weaknesses

Anti-Mage is very weak before Battle Fury (~22–25 minutes). He has low HP and poor fighting ability in the early game. He is countered by heroes that can lock him down before he can Blink away.

### Anti-Mage Playstyle

Farm the safe lane and adjacent jungle. Avoid fights until Battle Fury. After Battle Fury, split-push two lanes simultaneously. Join fights only when the enemy is grouped and vulnerable to Mana Void burst.

### Anti-Mage Under Pressure

If the enemy applies lane pressure on Anti-Mage before minute 18, the correct play is to farm the jungle, pull camps, or switch to a different quadrant. Do not try to contest the lane with low HP or no items.

## Juggernaut

Juggernaut is a flexible carry with strong duelling power at level 6 (Omnislash). He is one of the safest carries due to Blade Fury spell immunity.

### Juggernaut Strengths

Blade Fury grants spell immunity and deals good DPS, making Juggernaut difficult to burst in early skirmishes. Omnislash is a game-winning ultimate at level 6 against solo targets.

### Juggernaut Weaknesses

Juggernaut at low HP is very vulnerable because Blade Fury does not protect against physical damage when cast defensively. His laning is average and he needs early game support to succeed.

### Juggernaut Low HP Situation

If Juggernaut falls below 35% HP during a fight, activate Blade Fury immediately and use the spell-immunity window to retreat, not to deal damage. Prioritise survival over kills.

### Juggernaut Playstyle

Farm safely in lane, use Healing Ward after fights, purchase Diffusal Blade or Maelstrom depending on match-up. Join fights confidently at level 6 with Omnislash available.

## Luna

Luna is a farming carry who transitions into a teamfighter with Eclipse. She has the highest attack speed in the game at full Moon Glaive bounce and excels at pushing towers.

### Luna Strengths

Moon Glaives allow Luna to farm jungle camps and lane creeps simultaneously at high efficiency. Lucent Beam provides reliable single-target disable and damage.

### Luna Weaknesses

Luna is squishy and has no reliable escape mechanism. She is vulnerable to gap-closing heroes and loses fights in tight spaces where Moon Glaives cannot bounce effectively.

### Luna Farm vs Fight Decision

Luna should prioritise farming until Manta Style or Eye of Skadi. If a fight erupts near a tier-2 or tier-3 tower, it is worth joining because Luna's Eclipse can turn the fight and the push simultaneously. Random fights in open areas away from objectives should be skipped.

### Luna Playstyle

Farm the safe lane, stack ancients, push towers with Moon Glaives. Eclipse during Roshan or high-ground sieges for maximum value. Position at max range and use Manta to dispel silences.
